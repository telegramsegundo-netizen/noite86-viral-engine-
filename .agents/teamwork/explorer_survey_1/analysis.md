1. **Camera Gear & Optics**:
   - `Hasselblad H6D-100c, 100mm f/2.2 HC lens` (for clean, crisp, distortion-free studio commercial product shots).

2. **Volumetric Lighting**:
   - `Bioluminescent dual-tone neon lighting, vibrant cyan key light at 45 degrees, hot magenta rim lighting, soft warm tungsten fill, specular highlights on wet reflective surfaces, volumetric atmospheric haze`.

   - `Placed on luxury Italian terrazzo tile with pink and black marble chips`, `Reflected on wet asphalt after a tropical Florida rainstorm`, `Studio dark gradient backdrop with subtle steam and blue-violet rim light`.
4. **Negative Prompting Standard (SDXL / Flux parameter guidance)**:
   - `text, watermark, logo, brand name, trademark, low quality, blurry, distorted, cheap plastic look, 3d render CGI, cartoonish, oversaturated flat colors, extra limbs, deformed geometry, artifacts`.

## 4. Legal Trademark Boundaries & IP Clearance Framework

### 4.1 Legal Analysis & Trademark Law Principles
Under both Brazilian Law (Lei de Propriedade Industrial nº 9.279/1996) and US Trademark Law (Lanham Act, 15 U.S.C. § 1051 et seq.), trademark protection prevents consumer confusion regarding the source or sponsorship of commercial goods.

**Take-Two Interactive and Rockstar Games vigorously defend their proprietary marks.**
However, **no corporate entity owns**:
1. The historical aesthetic of 1980s or 1990s Miami, Florida.
2. The Art Deco architectural movement or color palettes.
3. The Synthwave, Retrowave, Vaporwave, and Outrun musical and visual subcultures.
4. Generic depictions of palm trees, flamingos, ocean sunsets, or retro sports cars.

### 4.2 Prohibited vs. Safe Elements Matrix

| Category | ❌ Strictly Banned (Infringement Risk) | ✅ Permitted & Protected (Original Homage) |
|---|---|---|
| **Company & Game Marks** | "Rockstar Games", "R* logo", "Take-Two", "Grand Theft Auto", "GTA", "GTA VI", "GTA 6" | "Vice Coast", "Ocean Drive", "Retro Florida", "Sunstate '86", "80s Syndicate" |
| **Fictional City Names** | Exact game map logos, "Vice City" used as the direct product trademark brand | "Magic City", "Biscayne Bay", "South Beach", "Vice Coast", "Palmetto City" |
| **Game Characters** | "Lucia", "Jason", "Tommy Vercetti", "Lance Vance", "Ricardo Diaz", "Sonny Forelli" | Original fictional personas ("The Courier", "The Smuggler", "Nocturne Driver", "The Night Owl") |
| **In-Game Fictional Brands** | "Ammu-Nation", "Sprunk", "FlyUS", "eCola", "Love Fist", "Weazel News", "Cluckin' Bell" | Original parodies & generic retro brands ("Vice Rush", "Tropic Sun", "Sunstate Air", "Palm Wave") |

| **3D Models & Renders** | Direct game engine asset rips, screenshots of trailers, official promotional keyart | 100% original AI-generated renders (Flux/Midjourney/SDXL) trained on aesthetic tokens |
